About

Through projects, workshops, and networking events, our club seeks professional development for all of our members. As a student-run organization, we're always seeking partners, sponsors, and new networking opportunities to build our community.

  • September 2023

    Creation of the IT Managment Club

    Mason Coler-Dark establishes the ITM Club with fellow classmates and supportive faculty advisors, Kevin Rank and John Wee. The Discord server we currently use was also created at this time, laying the foundation for community interaction.

  • November 2023

    First ITM Event

    The ITM Club hosts its first public event, inviting guest speaker and BSU Alumni Rachel Hogan from Women Innovators (W.IN).

  • April 2024

    Collaboration with the C^4 Club

    The ITM Club teams up with the Competitive Cybersecurity and Cyber-physical Club (C^4) to participate in the NCAE CyberGames Competition. Together, they defend a network from ethical hackers and tackle various CTF challenges throughout the competition.

    Merger with C^4 Club
